# 🛡️ Northstar Medical Group — Active Directory & Identity Access Management Project

## Project Overview

This project is part of the **TotalThreat 7-Day Portfolio Challenge**, a hands-on IT and cybersecurity experience designed to simulate real-world business challenges.

During this challenge, I take on the role of a **Junior IT & Identity Support Specialist** at Northstar Medical Group (NMG), a fictional healthcare organization based in Seattle, Washington.

The goal is to build an Active Directory environment and establish a structured employee identity lifecycle process that improves account management, access control, and IT operations.

Rather than simply installing Windows Server, this project focuses on applying technical skills to solve realistic business problems.

## Business Scenario

Northstar Medical Group is a rapidly growing healthcare company experiencing difficulties managing employee identities and access permissions.

As the organization expanded, its identity management processes became increasingly inefficient.

### Identified Business Problems

1. **Manual employee onboarding:** New employee accounts are created manually, increasing delays and errors.
2. **Unorganized departments:** The company lacks a consistent departmental structure for managing identities.
3. **Inconsistent access assignments:** Employees receive permissions without standardized access management procedures.
4. **Overprivileged accounts:** Some users have more permissions than necessary for their job responsibilities.
5. **Compliance risks:** Weak identity management practices increase potential HIPAA and Protected Health Information (PHI) security risks.

These issues highlight the need for centralized identity management and stronger access control practices.

## My Role and Responsibilities

**Position:** Junior IT & Identity Support Specialist

**Organization:** Northstar Medical Group (Simulated)

**Mission:** Help rebuild the company's identity management environment and implement a basic employee lifecycle process.

My responsibilities include:

- Documenting technical implementation and results.
